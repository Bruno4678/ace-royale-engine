Why the bot levels play differently

The same equity, estimated 40 times per level. A Rookie uses 40 samples, a Shark 650: fewer samples, a noisier view of its own hand, and noisier decisions.

03 — House edge

Computed exactly, not copied from a textbook

Player return per unit bet, measured with the engine's own rule functions: exact enumeration where the game allows it, 10 million simulated rounds where it does not.

Finding

The bug the numbers found

Baccarat charges 5% on Banker wins, in whole chips. Rounded down, Banker bets of 5 to 19 chips paid no commission at all and returned +1.24% to the player: betting small on Banker beat the house. The engine now rounds up, and no bet size favours the player.

Blackjack basic strategy, derived

Dynamic programming over every hand state. 6 decks, S17, DAS, 3:2. Rows: your hand; columns: the dealer's upcard.

Card counting: +0.50% per true count

10 million rounds in a 6-deck shoe. The player's edge at each Hi-Lo true count, with its 95% confidence interval. It turns positive at about +0.3.

04 — Bet sizing

Kelly: grow fastest, or survive?

A Hi-Lo counter with 1,000 table minimums bets the Kelly fraction edge / variance of the bankroll when the count is favourable, and the minimum otherwise. Rounds are resampled from the 10 million simulated ones, and every strategy faces the same rounds.
