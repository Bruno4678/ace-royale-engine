    The bug the numbers found

    Baccarat charges 5% on Banker wins, in whole chips. Rounded down, Banker bets of 5 to 19 chips paid no commission and returned +1.24% to the player. The engine now rounds up. Move the slider: the gold line is the old rule, the green one is the engine's.

    Blackjack basic strategy, derived by dynamic programming

    6 decks, dealer stands on soft 17, double after split, 3:2. Rows: your hand. Columns: the dealer's upcard.

    Card counting: +0.50% edge per true count point

    10 million rounds in a 6-deck shoe. Each point is the player's edge at one Hi-Lo true count, with its 95% confidence interval.

    Kelly: grow fastest, or survive?

    A Hi-Lo counter with 1,000 table minimums bets the Kelly fraction edge / variance of the bankroll when the count is favourable, and the minimum otherwise. Rounds are resampled from the 10 million simulated ones, and every strategy faces the same rounds.
